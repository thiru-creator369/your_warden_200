How Your Warden AI Firewall Protects You

Dual-Approval Sync Active

When you ask Claude or other AI models to read a resume, contract, or PDF, attackers can secretly hide malicious instructions inside that document. Your Warden scans every file before Claude touches it, quarantining dangerous files until you approve them.

🤖 What is Prompt Injection?

Hidden directives (like "Ignore previous instructions") that deceive the AI into ignoring safety rules and acting on behalf of an attacker instead of you.

🕵️ What is Data Exfiltration?

Invisible links or web beacons (like webhook URLs) embedded in documents designed to secretly transmit your private chat context or answers to an external server.

⚡ Which Decision Should I Choose?

Always choose Allow Sanitized! It removes the dangerous attacker commands while releasing the genuine text to Claude safely.
